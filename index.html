त्रिकोणमिति सम्पूर्ण फॉर्मूला शीट

1. मूल परिभाषाएँ

समकोण त्रिभुज में

\(\sin\theta=\frac{\text{लंब}}{\text{कर्ण}}\)
\(\cos\theta=\frac{\text{आधार}}{\text{कर्ण}}\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{\text{लंब}}{\text{आधार}}\)
\(\cot\theta=\frac{\text{आधार}}{\text{लंब}}\)
\(\sec\theta=\frac{\text{कर्ण}}{\text{आधार}}\)
\(\csc\theta=\frac{\text{कर्ण}}{\text{लंब}}\)

इकाई वृत्त (Unit Circle)

\(\sin\theta=y\)
\(\cos\theta=x\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{y}{x}\)
\(\csc\theta=\frac1y\)
\(\sec\theta=\frac1x\)
\(\cot\theta=\frac{x}{y}\)

3. मूल सर्वसमिकाएँ

\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)
\(1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta\)
\(1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\)
\(\cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\)
\(\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}\)
\(\csc\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}\)
\(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta}\)

4. ऋणात्मक कोण और आवर्ती सूत्र

\(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)
\(\sec(-\theta)=\sec\theta\)
\(\csc(-\theta)=-\csc\theta\)
\(\cot(-\theta)=-\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(\theta+2n\pi)=\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(\theta+2n\pi)=\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(\theta+n\pi)=\tan\theta\)
\(\cot(\theta+n\pi)=\cot\theta\)

5. Allied / Reduction Angles

\(\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)
\(\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)
\(\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(90^\circ+\theta)=\cos\theta\)
\(\cos(90^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\tan(90^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(180^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)
\(\sin(180^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(180^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(180^\circ+\theta)=\tan\theta\)
\(\sin(270^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\)
\(\cos(270^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\tan(270^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\)
\(\cos(270^\circ+\theta)=\sin\theta\)
\(\tan(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(360^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(360^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)

6. योग और अंतर सूत्र

\(\sin(A\pm B)=\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B\)
\(\cos(A\pm B)=\cos A\cos B\mp\sin A\sin B\)
\(\tan(A\pm B)=\frac{\tan A\pm\tan B}{1\mp\tan A\tan B}\)

7. Double, Triple और Multiple Angle

\(\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta\)
\(\cos2\theta=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta\)
\(\cos2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1=1-2\sin^2\theta\)
\(\tan2\theta=\frac{2\tan\theta}{1-\tan^2\theta}\)
\(\sin3\theta=3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta\)
\(\cos3\theta=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta\)
\(\tan3\theta=\frac{3t-t^3}{1-3t^2},\ t=\tan\theta\)
\(\sin n\theta,\cos n\theta\): De Moivre / complex-number expansion

8. Half Angle और Power Reduction

\(\sin^2\theta=\frac{1-\cos2\theta}{2}\)
\(\cos^2\theta=\frac{1+\cos2\theta}{2}\)
\(\tan^2\theta=\frac{1-\cos2\theta}{1+\cos2\theta}\)
\(\sin^3\theta=\frac{3\sin\theta-\sin3\theta}{4}\)
\(\cos^3\theta=\frac{3\cos\theta+\cos3\theta}{4}\)
\(\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac12\sin2\theta\)

9. Product ↔ Sum सूत्र

\(\sin A\sin B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)]\)
\(\cos A\cos B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)]\)
\(\sin A\cos B=\frac12[\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)]\)
\(\cos A\sin B=\frac12[\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)]\)
\(\sin A+\sin B=2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)

10. t = tan(θ/2) Universal Substitution

\(\sin\theta=\frac{2t}{1+t^2}\)
\(\cos\theta=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{2t}{1-t^2}\)
\(d\theta=\frac{2\,dt}{1+t^2}\)

12. General Trigonometric Equations

\(\sin x=\sin\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha\)
\(\cos x=\cos\alpha\Rightarrow x=2n\pi\pm\alpha\)
\(\tan x=\tan\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+\alpha\)
\(\cot x=\cot\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+\alpha\)
\(n\in\mathbb Z\)

13. Maximum / Minimum

\(a\sin x+b\cos x=R\sin(x+\phi)\)
\(R=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\)
Maximum \(=R\), Minimum \(=-R\)
\(\tan\phi=\frac{b}{a}\) (quadrant as required)
\(a\sin x+b\cos x+c\): range \([c-R,c+R]\)

14. Triangle Trigonometry

\(A+B+C=\pi\)
\(\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}=2R\)
\(a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A\)
\(b^2=c^2+a^2-2ca\cos B\)
\(c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C\)
\(\Delta=\frac12ab\sin C\)
\(\Delta=\frac{abc}{4R}\)
\(\Delta=rs\)
\(s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}\)
\(\Delta=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\)
\(r=\frac{\Delta}{s}\)
\(R=\frac{abc}{4\Delta}\)

15. Degree ↔ Radian

\(\pi\text{ rad}=180^\circ\)
\(1^\circ=\frac{\pi}{180}\text{ rad}\)
\(1\text{ rad}=\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\)
\(r=\frac{\pi\theta^\circ}{180^\circ}\)

16. Graph / Period Rules

\(y=A\sin(Bx+C)+D\): Amplitude \(=|A|\)
Period \(=\frac{2\pi}{|B|}\)
\(y=A\cos(Bx+C)+D\): Amplitude \(=|A|\)
\(y=A\tan(Bx+C)+D\): Period \(=\frac{\pi}{|B|}\)
Phase shift \(=-\frac{C}{B}\), vertical shift \(=D\)

20. Complete JEE + Class 12 Formula Bank — जो कुछ भी काम आता है

🔹 Basic + Reciprocal + Quotient

\(\sin\theta=\frac{\text{P}}{\text{H}},\quad \cos\theta=\frac{\text{B}}{\text{H}},\quad \tan\theta=\frac{\text{P}}{\text{B}}\)
\(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta},\quad \sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta},\quad \csc\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta},\quad \cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\)
\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)
\(\sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta=1\)
\(\csc^2\theta-\cot^2\theta=1\)

🔹 Complementary / Allied / Quadrant

\(\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)
\(\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)
\(\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\)
\(\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\)
\(\tan(180^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)
\(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(360^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)

🔹 Sum / Difference — All Useful Forms

\(\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B\)
\(\sin(A-B)=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B\)
\(\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B\)
\(\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B\)
\(\tan(A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}\)
\(\tan(A-B)=\frac{\tan A-\tan B}{1+\tan A\tan B}\)

🔹 Double / Triple / Quadruple

\(\sin2A=2\sin A\cos A\)
\(\cos2A=1-2\sin^2A=2\cos^2A-1\)
\(\tan2A=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A}\)
\(\sin3A=3\sin A-4\sin^3A\)
\(\cos3A=4\cos^3A-3\cos A\)
\(\tan3A=\frac{3t-t^3}{1-3t^2},\ t=\tan A\)
\(\cos4A=8\cos^4A-8\cos^2A+1\)
\(\sin4A=4\sin A\cos A(\cos^2A-\sin^2A)\)

🔹 Half Angle / Sub-multiple

\(\sin\frac A2=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos A}{2}}\)
\(\cos\frac A2=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos A}{2}}\)
\(\tan\frac A2=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos A}{1+\cos A}}\)
\(\tan\frac A2=\frac{\sin A}{1+\cos A}=\frac{1-\cos A}{\sin A}\)

🔹 Power Reduction / Powers

\(\sin^2A=\frac{1-\cos2A}{2}\)
\(\cos^2A=\frac{1+\cos2A}{2}\)
\(\sin A\cos A=\frac12\sin2A\)
\(\sin^3A=\frac{3\sin A-\sin3A}{4}\)
\(\cos^3A=\frac{3\cos A+\cos3A}{4}\)
\(\sin^4A=\frac{3-4\cos2A+\cos4A}{8}\)
\(\cos^4A=\frac{3+4\cos2A+\cos4A}{8}\)

🔹 Product ↔ Sum / Difference

\(\sin A\sin B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)]\)
\(\cos A\cos B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)]\)
\(\sin A\cos B=\frac12[\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)]\)
\(\sin A+\sin B=2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)
\(\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)

🔹 General Equations

\(\sin x=\sin\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha\)
\(\cos x=\cos\alpha\Rightarrow x=2n\pi\pm\alpha\)
\(\tan x=\tan\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+\alpha\)
\(\cot x=\cot\alpha\Rightarrow x=n\pi+\alpha\)
\(\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=n\pi\)
\(\cos x=0\Rightarrow x=\frac{(2n+1)\pi}{2}\)
\(\tan x=0\Rightarrow x=n\pi\)
\(n\in\mathbb Z\)

🔹 Transformation / Factorisation

\(\sin^2A-\sin^2B=\sin(A+B)\sin(A-B)\)
\(\cos^2A-\cos^2B=-\sin(A+B)\sin(A-B)\)
\(\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B=\sin(A+B)\)
\(\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B=\sin(A-B)\)
\(\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B=\cos(A+B)\)
\(\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)\)
\(\sin3A=4\sin A\sin(60^\circ+A)\sin(60^\circ-A)\)
\(\cos3A=4\cos A\cos(60^\circ+A)\cos(60^\circ-A)\)

🔹 R-form / Maximum-Minimum

\(a\sin x+b\cos x=R\sin(x+\phi)\)
\(R=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\)
\(\cos\phi=\frac aR,\quad \sin\phi=\frac bR\)
\(-R\le a\sin x+b\cos x\le R\)
Range of \(a\sin x+b\cos x+c=[c-R,c+R]\)

🔹 Trigonometric Inequalities

\(-1\le\sin x\le1\)
\(-1\le\cos x\le1\)
\(\tan x\) has no finite upper/lower bound
Use unit-circle sign + period to solve interval inequalities
For \(a\sin x+b\cos x\), first convert to R-form

🔹 Number of Solutions

\(\sin x=k\) has solutions iff \(|k|\le1\)
\(\cos x=k\) has solutions iff \(|k|\le1\)
\(\tan x=k\) has one solution in every interval of length \(\pi\)
Always intersect the general solution with the asked interval

🔹 Universal Substitution

\(t=\tan\frac x2\)
\(\sin x=\frac{2t}{1+t^2}\)
\(\cos x=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\)
\(\tan x=\frac{2t}{1-t^2}\)
\(dx=\frac{2\,dt}{1+t^2}\)

🔹 Inverse Trigonometry

\(\sin^{-1}x+\cos^{-1}x=\frac\pi2\)
\(\tan^{-1}x+\cot^{-1}x=\frac\pi2\)
\(\tan^{-1}x+\tan^{-1}y=\tan^{-1}\frac{x+y}{1-xy}\) (principal-value conditions)
\(\tan^{-1}x-\tan^{-1}y=\tan^{-1}\frac{x-y}{1+xy}\) (principal-value conditions)
\(\tan^{-1}x=\frac12\sin^{-1}\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\) for \(x\ge0\)

🔹 Triangle Trigonometry

\(A+B+C=\pi\)
\(\frac a{\sin A}=\frac b{\sin B}=\frac c{\sin C}=2R\)
\(a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A\)
\(\Delta=\frac12bc\sin A=\frac{abc}{4R}=rs\)
\(s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}\)
\(\Delta=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\)
\(r=\frac{\Delta}{s},\quad R=\frac{abc}{4\Delta}\)
\(\sin\frac A2=\sqrt{\frac{(s-b)(s-c)}{bc}}\)
\(\cos\frac A2=\sqrt{\frac{s(s-a)}{bc}}\)

🔹 Advanced Triangle Identities

\(\cos A+\cos B+\cos C=1+\frac rR\)
\(\sin^2\frac A2+\sin^2\frac B2+\sin^2\frac C2=2-\frac rR\)
\(\cos^2\frac A2+\cos^2\frac B2+\cos^2\frac C2=1+\frac rR\)
\(\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)

🔹 Complex Numbers / De Moivre Connection

\(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta=e^{i\theta}\)
\((\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos n\theta+i\sin n\theta\)
\(\cos n\theta=\Re[(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n]\)
\(\sin n\theta=\Im[(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n]\)

🔹 Graph / Period / Amplitude

\(y=a\sin(bx+c)+d\)
Amplitude \(=|a|\)
Period \(=\frac{2\pi}{|b|}\)
Phase shift \(=-\frac cb\)
Vertical shift \(=d\)
For tan: Period \(=\frac{\pi}{|b|}\)

🔹 Degree / Radian + Arc

\(180^\circ=\pi\text{ rad}\)
\(\theta_{\rm rad}=\theta^\circ\frac{\pi}{180}\)
\(s=r\theta\) (θ in radians)
Sector area \(=\frac12r^2\theta\)
